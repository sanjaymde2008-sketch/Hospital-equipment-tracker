        How staff ID + equipment works

        1) Staff taps their ID card on a reader. 2) Within 10 seconds, equipment tapped on the same reader is registered in that staff's name (Taken). 3) Equipment tapped without an ID card shows only as in use, user unknown. 4) Tapping equipment that is in use returns it at that reader.
